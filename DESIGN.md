## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a prestigious, archival, and contemporary industrial legacy environment. Tailored to honor the biographical narratives, humanitarian innovations, and engineering milestones of Simranjit Singh Bimbrah and the Bimbrah family, the aesthetic pairs the authority of historical documentation with the refined clarity of modern editorial design.

The target audience spans institutional partners, defense and engineering stakeholders (such as DRDO collaborators), civic dignitaries, and historical researchers. The visual tone evokes unshakeable credibility, quiet prestige, and deep cultural reverence.

Combining **Editorial Archival Modernism** with an **Architectural Card Architecture**, the system avoids nostalgic clutter in favor of crisp whitespace, balanced proportions, and deliberate gold accents that feel forged rather than decorative.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep midnight navy tones against tactile, warm metallics and crisp architectural backdrops.

- **Primary (`#1C2541`)**: Deep Naval Slate. Represents engineering rigor, institutional permanence, and authoritative headlines.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Burnished Champagne Gold. Reserved for insignia ribbons, chronological indicators, fine borders, and key calls-to-action.
- **Tertiary (`#0B132B`)**: Abyssal Midnight Navy. Used for deep-contrast text rendering, high-emphasis framing, and prominent footers.
- **Neutral (`#F8F9FA`)**: Alabaster Card Tint. Provides a pristine, high-legibility foundation that separates modern editorial clippings from generic flat white backgrounds.
- **Supporting Neutral Text (`#2D3748`)**: Deep Charcoal. Applied across long-form biographical text to maintain optical comfort across prolonged reading sessions.

## Typography

The type system balances transitional serif gravity with the precision of contemporary sans-serif geometric construction.

- **Headlines & Narrative Quotes**: Set in `Playfair Display`. Its sharp bracketed serifs and high stroke contrast echo historical broadsheets, patents, and honorific tributes.
- **Biographical Body & Archival Captions**: Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Delivers exceptional legibility for long-form chronicles, engineering dossiers, and news transcriptions.
- **Labels & Ribbons**: Rendered in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em`) to mirror luxury catalog and museum plaque engravings.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid provides cadence and proportion, accommodating both panoramic engineering photography and multi-column press archives.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` section margins. Editorial sections utilize an asymmetric 8:4 column split (narrative timeline on the left, archival assets and artifacts on the right).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with dynamic reflow, collapsing metadata to secondary sidebars.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Press clippings and photo cards collapse into vertical sequence cards with anchored metadata ribbons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and soft ambient drop shadows rather than aggressive elevation tiers.

- **Base Canvas**: Solid `#FFFFFF` or softened `#F8F9FA` tone.
- **Surface Tier (Cards & Press Clippings)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface framed by an ultra-thin 1px border tinted in `#1C2541` at 10% opacity, complemented by an ambient shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.05)`).
- **Interactive Hover State**: Translates vertically by `-2px` with shadow expansion (`0 12px 28px -4px rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.10)`), accompanied by a subtle shift to a burnished gold border (`#C5A059`).
- **Archival Overlay Framing**: Monochromatic photo frames employ double-layered keyline borders (1px gold inset, 4px white matte spacer) to evoke framed museum gallery displays.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tailored architectural aesthetic with subtle corner radiuses (`roundedness: 1`).

- Core UI elements (buttons, inputs, status tags) feature a delicate `0.25rem` (4px) curve, conveying modern refinement while retaining industrial precision.
- Editorial card modules, archival news boards, and modal containers use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Badges and category chips maintain crisp, non-pill geometric profiles to honor the formal print layout traditions seen in newspaper press clippings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Legacy Action**: Filled in `#1C2541` with `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, and a 1px border in `#C5A059` at 30% opacity. Focus and hover states warm the background toward `#0B132B` with crisp gold highlights.
- **Secondary / Archival Outline**: Transparent background with a 1.5px solid `#C5A059` border and `#1C2541` text. On hover, fills with `#F8F9FA` and deepens the border to `#B38E46`.

### Press & Biographical Cards
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`.
- Includes an integrated top "Badge Ribbon" (`#C5A059` background with `#FFFFFF` text, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`) to denote publication name, patent code, or honorific date.
- Employs an editorial matte photo frame with a 4px pure white border around imagery before encountering the card boundary.

### Metadata Chips & Ribbons
- Compact rectilinear tags with `0.25rem` radius. Tinted with a 10% wash of `#1C2541` with primary navy text, or a 12% wash of `#C5A059` for state honors and DRDO milestones.

### Lists & Timelines
- Milestone timelines utilize a continuous 1.5px vertical line in `#C5A059` (40% opacity). Active nodes are punctuated by dual concentric gold-and-navy geometric rings.
- Bulleted lists in narrative copy replace standard glyphs with small gold diamond icons (`0.35rem` square rotated 45 degrees).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form inputs feature crisp off-white (`#F8F9FA`) surfaces, deep navy text, and 1px borders in `#CBD5E1`. Active focus states engage a 1.5px `#C5A059` border with a subtle zero-spread focus ring.
- Checkboxes and radios are constructed with razor-thin geometry, filling with `#1C2541` and `#C5A059` check marks.
