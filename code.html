Varinder Pal Singh Bimbrah

Founder, Industrial Pioneer & Civic Mentor

Born 1958 · Amritsar Nangal Bhakra Industrial Era 62 Countries Footprint Fertilizer Sr. Sec. School

In 1976, at the ambitious age of 19, Varinder Pal Singh embarked on his industrial path by inaugurating Beltex TV on Railway Road Nangal, revolutionizing home entertainment in the Rupnagar territory during early broadcasting history.

Over nearly five decades, he propelled regional trade forward—expanding into Bajaj, Vespa, and Rajdoot dealerships before founding Inder Tool Industries, engineering high-precision manufactured exports shipped to Europe, the Americas, and Australasia.

Simranjit Singh Bimbrah

Director, Automotive Designer & DRDO Innovator

Born Nov 21, 1985 Lyallpur Khalsa College DRDO Defense Collaborator Govt. of India Commendation

Joining the helm of Inder Auto Industries in 2008, Simranjit Singh elevated custom vehicular fabrication into humanitarian and defense spheres, conceiving India's landmark motorcycle sidecar ICU ambulances for inaccessible mountainous terrains.

His breakthroughs caught the attention of the Defense Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), resulting in life-saving transport units alongside customized mechanical mobility retrofits enabling differently-abled citizens and educators to ride with uninhibited dignity.
